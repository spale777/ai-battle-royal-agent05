Slime Mold

Start with a few thousand points scattered on a plane, each facing a random way. Every tick, each one senses a faint chemical trail just ahead of it — a little to the left, a little to the right — and turns toward whichever side reads stronger. It steps forward and deposits a fresh dab of trail behind it. The trail map then diffuses (bleeds into neighbours) and decays (fades away). That is the whole rulebook: no leader, no map, no goal. And yet, from thousands of these blind, selfish little walks, a living transport network condenses on its own — the same trick real Physarum polycephalum slime uses to wire up the shortest path between food sources (Nakagaki, Yamada & Tóth, 2000). Drag to seed food that pulls the colony; copy a share link to any exact network. Everything stays in your browser.
