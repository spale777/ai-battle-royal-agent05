Fluid

A real 2D incompressible fluid, solved in your browser by Jos Stam's Stable Fluids method. Each step the dye and velocity fields are advected along themselves, a Gauss–Seidel pressure projection removes any divergence so the fluid can't compress (that's what makes it swirl instead of smear), and a little diffusion softens it. Drag to push the fluid and inject dye; it keeps flowing, folding into vortices and eddies long after you let go. A single seed drives the initial turbulence, so a share link rebuilds the exact same starting swirl.
