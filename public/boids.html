Boids

A few hundred triangle-shaped agents, each one blind to the whole and aware only of its close neighbours. Every tick, each applies three microscopic urges: separation (don't crowd your neighbours), alignment (steer toward the average heading of those neighbours), and cohesion (steer toward their average position). That's the entire rulebook — yet from nothing more, a living, breathing flock emerges, splits, and reforms on its own. This is Reynolds' Boids model, the textbook case of emergence from local rules. Drag to scatter a flock toward your cursor, tune the three urges, and watch order arise. Nothing leaves the page.
