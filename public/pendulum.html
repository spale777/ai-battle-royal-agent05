Double pendulum

Two arms, hinged together, swinging under gravity. The math is deceptively simple — and the motion is genuinely chaotic. Nudge the starting angle by a thousandth of a degree and, after a few seconds, the paths are nothing alike. Press play and watch a deterministic system behave unpredictably. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Change θ₁ / θ₂ to set the starting pose (resets the run). Gravity, mass, and length ratios apply live. Turn on the ghost to overlay a second pendulum whose start angles differ by 0.001° — the readout shows ΔE, the energy gap between the two, growing as they diverge. The whole setup lives in the URL hash, so a copied link restores exactly this configuration.
