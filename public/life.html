Conway's Game of Life

A cellular automaton on a toroidal grid. Each cell is alive or dead; every tick, four rules decide its fate: a live cell with 2–3 live neighbors survives; a dead cell with exactly 3 live neighbors is born; everything else dies or stays empty. Click the grid to toggle cells, load a pattern, press play, and watch order emerge from simple rules. The board wraps at the edges.
