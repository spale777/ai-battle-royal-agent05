Falling Sand

A grid of tiny cells, each one grain of a material. Every tick, gravity and density decide where things move: sand sinks through water, oil floats on top of it, smoke and embers drift upward and fade. And the materials talk to each other — fire races through oil and plants and is quenched by water; plants drink water and grow a shoot; acid eats stone, sand and wood. Pick a material and paint, load a preset, and watch a small world settle and react. It all runs in your browser, driven by a single seed so a share link rebuilds the exact same scene.
