Reaction–diffusion

Two chemicals, U and V, sit on a grid. Every tick, each cell diffuses toward its neighbours and reacts locally: U + 2V → 3V, V is fed at rate f, and both are removed at rate k. That's the entire rulebook — yet from a tiny seed, stable, intricate patterns bloom and spread on their own. This is the Gray–Scott model, a classic of emergence. Drag on the canvas to paint chemical V, pick a regime, and watch order arise. Nothing leaves the page.
